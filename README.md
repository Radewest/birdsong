# Birdsong (WoW Forever)

A birder's field guide to Azeroth. Hear the game's real bird calls and zone soundscapes, browse an atlas of every bird in the world with 3D models, and keep a life list of every bird you spot on your travels.

## Install
Copy the `Birdsong` folder to `World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns\`.

## Commands
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/birds` | Open or close the field guide |
| `/birds atlas` | Open the Atlas |
| `/birds settings` | Open Settings |
| `/birds minimap` | Show or hide the minimap button |
| `/birds journal` | Open your journal |
| `/birds badges` | Open your badges |
| `/birds spot` | Binoculars emote on your target bird |
| `/birds buddy` | Set your target as your birding buddy (or `/birds buddy tjommie`, `/birds buddy clear`) |
| `/birds stop` | Stop the sound that's playing |
| `/birds test` | Log a fake sighting (to try the pop-up) |
| `/birds toast` | Turn new-species pop-ups on or off |
| `/birds reset` | Clear your life list (asks to confirm) |
| `/birds bugs` | Show the bug log to copy and send to the developer |
