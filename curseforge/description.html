Birdsong: a field guide to Azeroth

Turn every zone into a birding trip. Birdsong is a roleplay-friendly field guide for WoW Forever that plays the game's own bird calls, shows every bird in the world with a 3D model, and keeps a life list of everything you spot.

Features

  • Field Guide: every bird family with its real in-game calls (call, alarm, strike, wounded, last cry), where it lives, and a rotating 3D model.
  • Atlas: 58 birds across the world, zone by zone, with levels and how many you've seen. Show on map pins exactly where they live.
  • Life list: target or mouse over a bird to log it. New species get a Lifer! pop-up; the 8 rare birds get a big gold one.
  • Tooltips: "Lifer waiting!" or "seen 3x" on every bird you hover.
  • Journal: every sighting written down with zone, date and game time. Add your own notes or write a page.
  • Badges: 24 to earn, from First Feathers to Night Owl, Dawn Chorus, Big Day and Master of the Atlas.
  • Binoculars: a roleplay emote: "raises a pair of binoculars and spots a Strigid Owl."
  • Birding buddy: compare life lists with a friend and get a pop-up when they log a lifer. Works with Tjommie in one click.
  • Zones and Soundscapes: play each zone's ambience and music.

Commands

/birds opens the book. /birds help lists the rest. There's also a minimap button.

Notes

  • No audio is shipped: Birdsong plays the game's own sound files.
  • Only shows information, and logs sightings outside combat, in line with the Midnight add-on rules.
  • Bird data from the open-source cmangos classic-db and pfQuest projects.
  • Found a bug? Type /birds bugs and paste the report in a comment.
