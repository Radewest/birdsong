# Birdsong changelog

## v0.3.3 (2026-11-10)
- First release on CurseForge, published automatically from GitHub.

## v0.3.2 (2026-11-09)
- Discoveries: birds the Atlas didn't know about (like the Green Wing Macaw) are added to your Atlas as your own discoveries, with their zone and level, and count towards your life list and badges.
- Big Day now counts each kind of bird once, the same way as the rest of the life list.
- Atlas: call buttons use a smaller font so "Wounded" fits; the legend no longer runs off the edge.
- Settings: your buddy shows by name, without the long realm code.

## v0.3.1 (2026-11-09)
- New hand-painted icon (add-on list, minimap button, book, pop-ups).
- New round map pins with the same robin; birds you haven't seen are greyed out, rares glow gold.

## v0.3.0 (2026-11-09)
- Map pins: "Show on map" in the Atlas marks where a bird lives on the world map (or pin every bird in the zone you're viewing, in Settings).
- Rare birds: 8 named rares, shown in orange, with a big "Rare bird!" pop-up.
- Tooltips: mouse over a bird to see "Lifer waiting!" or how often you've seen it (and whether your buddy has).
- Binoculars: a "Spot" button on pop-ups and in the Atlas, and /birds spot for your target, does a roleplay emote.
- Journal tab: every sighting is written down with zone, date and game time; add your own notes or write a page.
- Badges tab: 24 badges, from First Feathers to Master of the Atlas, Night Owl, Dawn Chorus and Big Day.
- Birding buddy: compare life lists with a friend, see what they've seen in the Atlas, and get a pop-up when they log a lifer. "Use my tjommie" sets it up in one click.
- Zones tab: each zone's soundscape and music to play, with sourced credits.
- Pop-ups now queue instead of covering each other.
- Tabs moved to their own row; Settings in two columns.

## v0.2.1 (2026-11-09)
- New-species pop-up now says "Lifer!".
- Book window is fully solid, so names in the world no longer show through.
- Longer zone names fit in the Atlas list.

## v0.2.0 (2026-11-09)
- New Atlas tab: every bird in the classic world (58 kinds) by zone, with levels, where else it lives and how many you've seen.
- 3D models on the Field Guide and Atlas pages (drag to turn, scroll to zoom).
- Sightings now recognise the exact creature, so your life list tracks kinds as well as species; a smaller pop-up for each new kind.
- Field Guide shows where each bird really lives instead of hand-written guesses.
- Menu rework: Field Guide, Atlas, Soundscapes and Settings tabs.
- Settings tab (also in Options > AddOns): pop-ups, sound channel, model spin, minimap button, chat, clear life list.
- Minimap button: left-click for the book, right-click for settings, drag to move.
- New commands: /birds atlas, /birds settings, /birds minimap.

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-09)
- First test build: field guide with 12 birds and their real in-game calls, 18 soundscapes, life list from targeting/mousing over birds, new-species pop-up.
